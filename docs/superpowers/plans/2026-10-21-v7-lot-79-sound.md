## L79-01 — Add audio manager, Sound menu item and UI click

**Cx / risk:** M / Medium · **Depends on:** Lot 78 · **Spec:** §9, §4.2, §4.5, §5.6, §6.1, §7.8, §10.4, §10.6, §12.2, §12.5, §5.8
**Parity (Appendix A):** `global-client-storage`, `global-input-modalities`, `global-dialog-button-primitives`, `turn-strip-timers`, `table-fx-layer`
**Files:** `apps/client/src/ui/sound/audio-manager.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/audio-manager.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/sound-settings.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/sound-settings.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/clips.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/clips.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/sound-provider.tsx`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/sound-provider.test.tsx`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`, `apps/client/src/ui/primitives/`, `apps/client/src/screens/arena/`, `apps/client/src/App.tsx`, `packages/shared/src/release-notes.ts`, `packages/shared/src/release-notes.test.ts`, `docs/agent/frontend.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `docs/backlog_v7.md`

Add a `ui/sound/` audio manager on the native Web Audio API (no audio library, §4.5). It holds one `AudioContext` behind a master gain. Clips are registered in `ui/sound/clips.ts` (`import.meta.glob` `?url` over `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/`) and fetched and decoded once. `play(cueId)` is fire-and-forget: an intent never awaits it, it never throws, and a clip that fails to load stays silent while the other clips still play. Unlock on the first user gesture (§9): one passive capture listener at the game-app root (`pointerdown` / `keydown` / `touchend`) never prevents default and is not a hotkey. It resumes the context, retries on later gestures until the context runs, then removes itself. Cues requested before unlock are dropped, not queued. `/admin` mounts no sound (tool mode, §7.8). Settings live in `localStorage['card-battle.v7.sound']` = `{ volume, muted }`, default on at a named moderate volume (§9), read and written with the try/catch and fallback pattern of `help-storage.ts`. The arena top-bar menu (§6.1) gains **Sound** after its existing items (sound icon, §5.6): a mute toggle and a volume slider built from the L72 primitives, applied live and persisted. They stay usable on the finished board, for spectators and in the tutorial, because they are local settings, not intents. First cue: **UI click** on enabled arcade `Button` / `IconButton` presses inside the arena (scope is open, see questions). Commit the Kenney CC0 click clip and create `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`: one row per clip with committed file, cue, Kenney pack, URL, original file name, license CC0 1.0, and edits. `docs/agent/frontend.md` gains a Sound and haptics (V7) section: manager, unlock rule, storage key and defaults, and the rule that every new clip adds a CREDITS row in the same commit. `DESIGN.md` documents the Sound menu row. The open What's new entry gains a Sound Before / After item (§10.4). New strings (for example 'Volume', 'Mute') are listed in the PR (§10.6).

**Acceptance:** Fake-`AudioContext` tests: nothing plays before the first gesture; one gesture resumes once; muted or volume 0 starts no source; volume drives the master gain; a failed decode leaves the other clips playable. Settings tests: missing, corrupt or throwing storage falls back to the defaults, and writes are skipped when storage is unavailable. Component test (happy-dom): mute and slider update `card-battle.v7.sound` and the manager; a disabled Button plays no click; a Button's `onClick` still runs while audio is locked. A test fails when a committed clip is missing from `CREDITS.md` or a cue has no clip. Release-notes latest-id tests are updated if a new id opens. No `Date.now()` in tests. `pnpm verify` green.

**Review:** Downloading the Kenney CC0 packs needs the developer's explicit OK at execution time; the designer signs off the clip selection by listening in the PR. Volume default 60%.

## L79-02 — Play game-event sounds from the FX triggers

**Cx / risk:** M / Medium · **Depends on:** L79-01 · **Spec:** §9, §8.1, §8.3, §6.3, §6.6.2, §4.3, §10.4, §12.2
**Parity (Appendix A):** `table-fx-layer`, `table-fx-card-ghosts`, `table-fx-token-chips`, `table-fx-resolution-elim-reward`, `table-banners`, `global-motion-reduced-motion`
**Files:** `apps/client/src/view-model/sound-cues.ts`, `apps/client/src/view-model/sound-cues.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/screens/arena/`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/clips.ts`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`, `packages/shared/src/release-notes.ts`, `docs/agent/frontend.md`, `docs/backlog_v7.md`

A pure `view-model/sound-cues.ts` (React-free, no clock reads, §4.3) maps the §8.1 triggers that L78 already derives (`lastActionPlayed`, `lastActionResolved`, `lastTurnStarted`, view / `actionLog` diffs) to cue ids. The arena FX host plays them:
- **card swish**, once per card-play flight. The POV's optimistic flight plays it at send; the deduped `ACTION_PLAYED` for the POV's own instance plays nothing (§6.6.2).
- a hit on each attack `actionResolved` with outcome `applied`: **shield hit** when public `shieldAbsorbed > 0`, then **light** or **heavy** by public `livesLost` (threshold is open, see questions), in the §8.1 float order (shield first).
- **coins** on gains and steals, under the token-chip rules (none on `drawBust`, no amount beyond what the chips already show).
- **spy** when the Spy eye opens on a seat.
- **elimination** for each new elimination band, seeded on mount like `eliminationBeat` (Q63).
- **your turn** with the YOUR TURN slam.
- **victory** with the 'You won!' cue.

Sound reads only fields the POV's view or a broadcast message already carries, so it never tells more than the screen (§6.3). Identical triggers in one update play once; a fan-out play is one swish. Cues still fire under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, because §8.3 removes only visual motion; only Mute silences them. Commit the clips with their CREDITS rows. `docs/agent/frontend.md` gains the moment → clip table and the no-replay and visibility rules. The open What's new Sound item is widened to cover game sounds.

**Acceptance:** View-model tests:
- one cue per trigger
- shield-only, light and heavy hits
- no swish for the own-instance `ACTION_PLAYED`
- no coins on `drawBust`
- no cue for elimination history on mount, on reconnect or on the finished board
- cues unchanged under reduced motion
- duplicate triggers in one update collapse to one

Component test with a mocked manager: a resolved hit on the POV plays the hit cue, and the intent path never awaits audio. `pnpm verify` green.

**Review:** Hit sounds only for attacks to or from the POV; heavy when `shieldAbsorbed + livesLost` ≥ 7.

## L79-03 — Tick the last five seconds of the turn clock

**Cx / risk:** S / Low · **Depends on:** L79-01 · **Spec:** §9, §6.10, §8.1, §4.3, §10.4
**Parity (Appendix A):** `table-turn-timer-scale`, `turn-strip-timers`
**Files:** `apps/client/src/view-model/clock-tick.ts`, `apps/client/src/view-model/clock-tick.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/screens/arena/`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/clips.ts`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/`, `apps/client/src/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`, `packages/shared/src/release-notes.ts`, `docs/agent/frontend.md`, `docs/backlog_v7.md`

A pure `view-model/clock-tick.ts` reads the `turnClock()` output (L73, §6.10) and returns the whole second to tick (5 down to 1), only in **countdown** mode. The arena top-bar clock plays the **clock tick** cue once per new second per `turnSequence` (§8.1 Clock row). It never ticks in **paused**, **absent** or **none** mode (tutorial, finished board, bot turns, null deadline). A resumed `TURN_STARTED` or a throttled tab plays only the current second, never a burst of missed ones. Display stays cosmetic: ticks follow the server deadline through `turnClock`, and nothing is sent. Commit the tick clip with its CREDITS row. `docs/agent/frontend.md` states the tick rule. The open What's new Sound item is updated if its sentence no longer covers the tick.

**Acceptance:** View-model tests per mode, with `nowMs` passed in (no `Date.now()`):
- countdown ticks 5 to 1 once each
- paused, absent and none never tick
- a jump from 7 s to 3 s ticks once, at 3
- a second `TURN_STARTED` for the same `turnSequence` does not repeat seconds already ticked

`pnpm verify` green.

**Review:** Tick only on the POV's own turn.

## L79-04 — Add haptics and the Vibration menu item

**Cx / risk:** S / Low · **Depends on:** L79-01 · **Spec:** §9, §6.1, §8.1, §5.6, §11, §10.4, §10.6, §12.2, §5.8
**Parity (Appendix A):** `global-client-storage`, `table-banners`, `table-fx-layer`
**Files:** `apps/client/src/ui/sound/haptics.ts`, `apps/client/src/ui/sound/haptics.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/view-model/haptic-cues.ts`, `apps/client/src/view-model/haptic-cues.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/screens/arena/`, `packages/shared/src/release-notes.ts`, `docs/agent/frontend.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `docs/backlog_v7.md`

`ui/sound/haptics.ts` wraps `navigator.vibrate` behind feature detection (iOS Safari has none, §9). It is gated on the same first-gesture unlock as sound and uses short named pulse constants. A pure `view-model/haptic-cues.ts` gives one pulse when the POV's turn starts (same trigger as the YOUR TURN slam) and one when an attack resolves on the POV with outcome `applied` (the POV hit of §8.1), whatever the reduced-motion setting. The setting lives in `localStorage['card-battle.v7.haptics']` with the try/catch and fallback pattern. Default is on, mirroring Sound (to confirm, see questions). It is separate from Sound: muting sound never stops vibration, and turning vibration off never mutes sound. The arena top-bar menu gains **Vibration** after Sound (vibration icon, §5.6) as a toggle. Where vibration is unsupported, the toggle shows as disabled with its reason (§11 pattern; copy is open, see questions). `docs/agent/frontend.md` gets a haptics paragraph (key, default, triggers, pulse constants). `DESIGN.md` documents the Vibration row. The open What's new entry gains a Vibration Before / After item. The new string is listed in the PR.

**Acceptance:** Tests with a stubbed `navigator.vibrate`:
- the POV turn start and a POV hit pulse once each
- a hit on another seat does not pulse
- disabled or unsupported never calls `vibrate`
- no call before the first gesture
- muting sound leaves vibration on

Settings fallback tests. Component test (happy-dom): toggling Vibration writes `card-battle.v7.haptics`, and without `navigator.vibrate` the item renders disabled with its reason. `pnpm verify` green.

**Review:** Vibration defaults on, hidden where unsupported; pulses on the POV's turn and on attack hits only.

## Draft questions and their dispositions

Resolved in the 2026-10-21 backlog review unless listed under "Designer questions still open" in `docs/backlog_v7.md`; dispositions are recorded in the **Review** lines above and in the amended spec.

- UI click scope: §9 lists a UI click clip but does not say where it plays, and Sound / Vibration settings exist only in the table menu (§6.1; Home has no menu, §7.1). The draft plays the click only inside the arena. Should it play app-wide (Home, lobby, Game over)? If so, where does a player mute before joining a game?
- Light vs heavy hit: §8.1 scales shake to damage but sets no threshold. Proposed default: heavy when the public `livesLost` is at least 7 (Super Attack base and MEGA ATTACK), else light. Or should the sound reuse whatever tiers L78 picks for screen shake?
- Hits between other seats: should every resolved attack play a hit sound, as the hit flash shows on any seat? Or only attacks to or from the POV, to keep an 8-seat table quiet?
- Default volume: §9 says 'on at moderate volume'. Proposed: 60% of full gain. Please confirm or give a value.
- Haptics default: §9 gives a default only for sound. Should Vibration also default to on (draft) or to off?
- Vibration where unsupported (iOS Safari, desktop): hide the menu item, or show it disabled with a reason such as 'Not available on this device' (draft, new string)?
- Clock tick: §6.10 / §8.1 tick the last 5 s in countdown mode, which includes other humans' turns. Keep that (draft), or tick only on the POV's own turn?
- Haptic 'when hit': attack resolutions only, matching the §8.1 'Attack resolves' row (draft)? Or also non-attack life loss (Tax, Poison tick, Curse, Imposition)?
- Reduced motion: the draft keeps sounds and vibration under prefers-reduced-motion, since §8.3 removes only visual motion and Mute is the sound control. Please confirm.
- Clip sourcing and sign-off: may the implementing session download the Kenney CC0 packs from kenney.nl (it needs the developer's explicit OK to download)? Which packs are preferred? Does the designer sign off the clip selection by listening in the PR, like the L72 art contact sheet?
- Audio format and iOS check: Kenney ships OGG, and older iOS Safari may not decode OGG in Web Audio. Commit OGG only, or commit MP3/M4A conversions (a one-off conversion, no runtime dependency)? The agent's gate browser is Chromium, so can iOS unlock and playback be confirmed on a real iPhone through the staging preview?
